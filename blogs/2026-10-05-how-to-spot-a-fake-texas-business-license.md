In May 2026, a Dallas resident was convicted of running coin-operated “amusement machines” without a business license, according to the Texas Comptroller . The penalty was six months of community supervision, a $500 fine, and $310 in court costs. Two months later, a Houston resident was arrested on the same charge after seven unlicensed machines were found at his business.
