Within the fake document ecosystem, things can change fast. Four months ago, we reported that OnlyFake , the infamous fake document generator, had rebranded itself as “MacDoc" after the FBI announced it arrested one of the service’s original operators, Yurii Nazarenko . New logo, new visual identity, new website domains, but the same generator engine and the same document catalog.
