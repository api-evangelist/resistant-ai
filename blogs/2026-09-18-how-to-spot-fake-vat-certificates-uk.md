In 2026, HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) put the UK VAT gap , the difference between the value-added tax (VAT) it should collect and what it actually receives, at £12.1 billion for 2024–25. Fraud could be one of the primary drivers behind that gap. For example, a fake VAT registration certificate is how a business can appear VAT registered while avoiding the tax altogether.
