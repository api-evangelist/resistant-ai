Application fraud in 2026 is easier to attempt: accessible tools and services help applicants turn false claims into convincing applications. Equifax’s September 2026 findings show a 5% year-over-year increase in the overall consumer application fraud rate in Canada in Q2 2026. Methods included identity theft and misrepresentation of income, employment, or assets.
